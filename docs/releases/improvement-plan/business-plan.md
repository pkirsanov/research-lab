# Business Plan - `improvement-plan` phase

## Phase Objective

Improve decision quality and the measurement of decision quality for one discretionary investor. This phase is
not a commercialization launch. It closes publication, coverage, scoring, and record-integrity gaps in the
existing static product.

## Audience

| Audience | Need | Phase value |
|---|---|---|
| Single operator | One place to prepare, inspect, record, and review market-research decisions | A coherent scheduled brief, watchlist depth, scoreable claims, and append-only outcomes |
| Public technical reader | A way to judge the analysis without trusting promotional copy | Visible provenance, explicit gaps, misses beside hits, and an honest error rate |
| Contributor or reviewer | A reliable map from product promise to delivered evidence | Current release bindings, exact acceptance metrics, and unresolved findings routed by owner |

The phase does not target institutional teams, paying subscribers, brokerage customers, or multi-user
workflows. Those would conflict with the current single-operator and no-execution boundaries.

## Value Proposition

Research Lab does not compete on the number of charts. It combines owning tools, a global Market Action
Center, machine-checkable recommendations, and a public outcome record. The value of this phase is that those
parts agree under scheduled operation:

1. publication is atomic;
2. watched tickers receive supported reads and explicit gaps;
3. every new tactical or swing proposal is scoreable or withheld;
4. historical outcomes remain append-only;
5. product and release records state the same delivery truth.

## Product Stage

The current phase is **in progress**. At local repository planning snapshot
`eac966b78bacc5697458a7c8e54a684f6517c05b` (2026-08-12), the repository had 25 live registry entries, 52
journey definitions, and 67 journey steps. Those are snapshot inventory facts, not a claim that the active
release is ready. The focused atomicity suite is red, scheduled owner-read publication remains unresolved, and
several required bindings remain non-terminal or uncertified. Scheduled origin artifacts advance independently;
execution and release remeasure them at their own date and revision.

## Commercial Posture

There is no owner-approved paid offer, pricing tier, customer-acquisition budget, sales motion, financing
request, or revenue target in the current direction artifacts. The honest phase posture is:

- public educational product;
- no investment advice;
- no brokerage or execution;
- no commercial availability claim;
- no revenue forecast.

Any commercial experiment requires a separate owner decision and must preserve public error-rate integrity.
It cannot create an incentive to hide misses or weaken unavailable states.

## Competition Analysis

No named-competitor comparison is asserted in this packet. No current-session market research was performed,
and the repository direction does not provide a current, source-qualified competitor matrix. Inventing one
would violate the release truth standard.

The only bounded product-positioning comparison supported here is internal: a conventional dashboard can show
numbers, while Research Lab's intended differentiation is to show the claim, its evidence, its invalidation,
and its realised outcome. This statement describes Research Lab's design goal. It is not a claim about any
named competitor's current product.

**Owner decision before commercial positioning:** authorize a dated market-research pass with named sources,
then evaluate whether the public error-rate posture is genuinely differentiated.

## Success Decision

The phase creates business value only if the reader can trust the record. The release decision therefore
follows the technical evidence in `vision.md`, `features.md`, and `actions.md`; tool count, sunk effort, or
visual polish cannot override a red atomicity suite or incomplete required binding.
