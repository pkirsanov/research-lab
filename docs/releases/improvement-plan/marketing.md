## Claim Guardrails

Marketing must not claim:

- that 25 live registry entries means 25 fully certified workflows;
- that the registry is already 29;
- that the rolling blind-spot KPI has met target;
- that the planning snapshot's 45 of 45 recent machine-checkable proposals repairs historical calls;
- that Feature 004 is integrated into scheduled composition merely because its spec is certified;
- that bond or smart-money evidence is current when the brief says stale;
- that Research Lab predicts returns, provides advice, executes orders, or guarantees outcomes;
- that G101 alone proves current integration health.

Every launch metric must carry its measurement date, repository revision, denominator, and relevant sample
qualification. The scorecard planning baseline is fixed at local commit
`eac966b78bacc5697458a7c8e54a684f6517c05b` (2026-08-12): 246 closed, 96 resolved, 143 not evaluable,
`notEvaluableShare` 0.5813, and hit rate 0.5729. The latest origin scorecard has advanced independently; launch
copy must remeasure it on the release revision rather than silently replacing this baseline.

## Measurement

Launch effectiveness is not defined as page views or sign-ups in this phase. The accepted measures are product
truth measures:

- required release checks pass;
- current claims include dates, repository revisions, denominators, and sample qualifications;
- no unsupported capability appears in launch copy;
- readers can inspect misses, not-evaluable share, and evidence gaps;
- no launch asset implies investment advice or execution.
