# Monetization - `improvement-plan` phase

## Current Revenue Posture

**No monetization model is approved for this phase.** Research Lab is a public, static, single-operator
educational product. The repository provides no current pricing decision, paid tier, customer count,
acquisition cost, conversion rate, revenue target, or financing plan.

The phase therefore uses an honest **N/A** posture rather than inventing commercial assumptions.

## Why Monetization Is Not A Phase Goal

The product's differentiator depends on publishing misses, blind spots, and insufficient samples. A premature
commercial model could create pressure to suppress exactly those outcomes. Before considering revenue, the
owner must establish that the honesty loop works under scheduled publication and remains visible when results
are unfavourable.

The admission test still applies: a commercial feature must improve decision quality or its measurement. A
paywall, account system, engagement mechanic, or premium badge does not pass by itself.

## Customer Acquisition

There is no customer-acquisition plan in this phase. Repository discovery and public Pages access may expose
the work to readers, but this packet does not reinterpret those readers as leads or customers.

Any later acquisition experiment must define consent, analytics boundaries, success metrics, and the effect on
public error-rate integrity before launch.

## Path To A Monetization Decision

A future owner review may consider monetization only after:

1. P0 scheduled-publication atomicity is green.
2. Required release bindings are delivered and validate-certified.
3. The 12-ticker owner-read path is stable under scheduled operation.
4. New-call evaluability remains corrected and the rolling scorecard converges without rewritten history.
5. The owner commissions dated competitor and willingness-to-pay research.
6. A proposed model preserves public misses, sample-size withholding, explicit unavailable states, and
   unconditional export of the public record.

Meeting these conditions authorizes a decision, not a paid launch.

## Revenue Timeline

## Monetization Risks

| Risk | Required control |
|---|---|
| Hiding misses to improve conversion | Misses and blind spots remain equally prominent and mechanically checked |
| Turning confidence into a performance promise | Confidence remains evidence quality; realised rates stay in the scorecard |
| Paywalling the audit trail | Public accountability posture requires an explicit owner decision before any restriction |
| Collecting private portfolio data | Tickers-only committed boundary remains absolute |
| Expanding into advice or execution | Product and legal boundary remains educational and no-execution |
| Adding accounts for billing | Multi-user and auth remain outside current direction |
