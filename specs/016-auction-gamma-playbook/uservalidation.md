## Checklist — Business Behaviors (BS-016-001 … BS-016-036)

- [x] With a balancing session and a suppressive regime whose flip is distant, my read of the lower value-area edge expects the edge to hold and price to rotate back toward the point of control, names both the balancing auction state and the suppressive regime as its basis, states a rotation trade shape and one falsifier I can check, and stamps every figure with its source, provenance class and as-of. (BS-016-001)
- [x] With the same balancing structure but an amplifying regime, the read tells me the edge is more likely to break than hold, names the amplifying regime as the reason the rotation expectation was downgraded, states an acceptance-below-the-edge trade shape, and states the observable condition that would restore the rotation case. (BS-016-002)
- [x] When price sits close to the gamma flip in a balancing session, the read calls the structure genuinely two-sided, claims neither that the edge holds nor that it breaks, names the flip proximity as the cause of the instability, gives me the condition on each side that would resolve it, and shows lower confidence than a comparable cell whose regime is stable. (BS-016-003)
- [x] With an imbalanced session and a suppressive regime, the read expects the extension to be dampened and to retrace toward value, names the suppressive regime as the reason the directional structure is qualified rather than confirmed, states a trade shape that respects the dampening, and states what would confirm genuine acceptance beyond the level. (BS-016-004)
- [x] With an imbalanced session and an amplifying regime, the read expects the level to break with a shallow retracement, names the auction state as the supplier of level and direction and the amplifying regime as the supplier of the expectation, states a continuation trade shape, and gives one falsifier that would end the continuation case. (BS-016-005)
- [x] With an imbalanced session near the gamma flip, the read gives me the direction and the level but withholds any dampened-or-extended expectation, names the flip proximity as the reason for withholding it inside that cell's own basis rather than only in a general disclosure, and shows lower confidence than a stable-regime comparable. (BS-016-006)
- [x] Reading the same level under a suppressive regime and under an amplifying regime gives me opposite expectations, each attributing the difference to its regime rather than to any structural difference, each carrying its own falsifier naming a different observation, so I can act on one while watching a single named condition. (BS-016-007)
- [x] When gamma context is ready but the session has not yet established an auction state, the gamma is presented as context only, no expectation, direction or trade shape is asserted, the absent auction state is named as the reason, and the read is distinguishable from one where an expectation was asserted. (BS-016-008)
- [x] Tracing a fully qualified cell, every level, target and direction traces back to an auction primitive and none traces to a gamma primitive; gamma appears only as a qualifier of the expectation. (BS-016-009)
- [x] When the session develops enough structure for the auction state to reach readiness under the same evidence cutoff, my context-only read becomes a fully qualified cell stating the expectation, the trade shape and the falsifier, and that change is visible to me. (BS-016-010)
- [x] When an auction state and a regime both qualify but no observable falsifier can be stated, no expectation is asserted for that pairing, the missing falsifier is named as the reason, and I am not shown a partial expectation with its falsifier stripped off. (BS-016-011)
- [x] Every asserted cell's falsifier names a level, a direction and a confirming condition, is phrased so I can check it against the developing session without re-deriving the model, and sits alongside the expectation rather than only in a separate disclosure. (BS-016-012)
- [x] Each asserted cell also states the observation that would indicate the behavioural regime itself has changed, stated distinctly from the falsifier of the individual expectation, so I can tell which of the two a given observation would trigger. (BS-016-013)
- [x] When no usable gamma evidence exists for a ticker whose auction state is ready, I get an explicitly auction-only expectation that names the missing input, its availability state and the reason it is unavailable, asserts no behavioural regime, and still carries its own falsifier. (BS-016-014)
- [x] A read with no usable gamma evidence shows the gamma half as unavailable and never substitutes a balanced, neutral or mid-range regime for the missing one, so it is distinguishable from a read with a measured stable regime without my inspecting the underlying evidence. (BS-016-015)
- [x] Scanning a mixed set, each reduced read is visually and structurally distinguishable from each fully qualified cell, no reduced read borrows the confidence presentation of a full cell, and each states its auction-only expectation, the named missing input and that input's reason. (BS-016-016)
- [x] When usable gamma evidence arrives under the same evidence cutoff, my reduced read re-qualifies into a fully asserted cell that states the newly available snapshot's as-of, and the reduced read stops presenting as the current read. (BS-016-017)
- [x] When the gamma snapshot's as-of falls outside the auction read's evidence cutoff, the gamma half is classified stale rather than ready, the read states the snapshot's as-of and the cutoff it failed, an auction-only reduced read is issued instead of a fused assertion, and no regime derived from that stale snapshot is asserted. (BS-016-018)
- [x] Inspecting a read whose gamma as-of fell outside the cutoff, the declared cutoff shown is the same one the auction half was asserted against, the stale input is excluded rather than admitted, and staleness is presented as staleness rather than resolved by relaxing the bound. (BS-016-019)
- [x] Any gamma-derived element sourced from a prior-session snapshot displays its snapshot as-of and is labelled a convention-dependent estimate from prior-session open interest rather than measured dealer positioning, and is never displayed as a live intraday reading. (BS-016-020)
- [x] Inspecting a fused cell's basis, each participating primitive keeps the provenance class it had before fusion — the bar-reconstructed input stays a model estimate, the up/down-volume input stays a proxy, the gamma input stays a convention-dependent estimate — and the fused cell carries no class stronger than any of its inputs. (BS-016-021)
- [x] A cell's stated confidence never exceeds what its least-qualified participating primitive supports, and the cell names which primitive bounds that confidence. (BS-016-022)
- [x] When a regime comes from a snapshot with thin usable contract coverage, the cell's confidence is bounded by that coverage quality, the limitation is named in that cell's own basis rather than only in a general disclosure, and the cell does not present with the confidence of a comparable well-covered cell. (BS-016-023)
- [x] When usable contract coverage falls below what a behavioural regime requires, the gamma half is presented as unavailable with coverage named as the reason, no regime is asserted, and I get an auction-only reduced read. (BS-016-024)
- [x] When price sits close enough to the gamma flip that modest movement would invert the modelled sign, the regime is presented as hinge-proximate rather than as suppressive or amplifying, the read names the flip distance that makes the sign unstable, and any expectation under it is stated as two-sided rather than directional. (BS-016-025)
- [x] When the modelled net-gamma profile changes sign outside the band sampled ten percent either side of spot, the flip is presented as not locatable within the modelled band, the read states that the search was bounded to that band rather than implying no flip exists, no flip distance is shown, and no regime is claimed on the basis of one. (BS-016-026)
- [x] For a ticker the published options snapshot set of twenty-two tickers does not cover, the read states that no same-origin gamma evidence exists, names the missing input and the reason, issues an auction-only reduced read rather than a regime derived from a substituted source, and asserts no behavioural regime. (BS-016-027)
- [x] The corroborating flow evidence built only from whether each bar closed at or above its open is labelled an up/down-volume proxy, is stated as not being bid/ask, depth or trade-level aggression data, appears as corroborating evidence only and never as the sole basis of an asserted expectation, and is never described or drawn as real order flow. (BS-016-028)
- [x] The value-area element is labelled a model estimate reconstructed from bars, states that intrabar distribution is not observed, states that bucket resolution scales with the session range so a wider-range session gives coarser price granularity, and is not presented as tick or time-price-opportunity data. (BS-016-029)
- [x] The early-session balance element states the window value it was computed against, states that the window is a declared parameter and not the classical initial-balance interval, states that the window resolves to a whole number of bars at the selected interval so the two interact, and visibly changes in the read when I change the declared window. (BS-016-030)
- [x] Moving through the tool's four top-level views, the playbook is expressed inside Simple, Power, Brief and Journey with no fifth top-level view, duplicate top-level toggle or parallel tool entry, and its expression in each view suits that view's purpose rather than repeating another view's content verbatim. (BS-016-031)
- [x] When I select the parameter value that excludes gamma context even though usable gamma evidence exists, the read gives an auction-only expectation, states that gamma context was excluded by my own parameter selection, asserts no behavioural regime, and still carries its own falsifier. (BS-016-032)
- [x] A read whose gamma half I excluded by parameter attributes the absence to my selection, while a read with no usable gamma evidence attributes it to the missing or disqualified evidence and names it; the two are distinguishable, and selecting the value that includes gamma context restores a fused cell for the first. (BS-016-033)
- [x] Returning after a completed session, each recovered assertion states what was expected, the evidence cutoff it was asserted against and its falsifier, I can determine whether each falsifier triggered during the session, and recording an outcome does not alter the original assertion or its falsifier. (BS-016-034)
- [x] A cell invalidated by a mid-session regime change is recorded as invalidated by a regime change, distinguishable from an expectation whose falsifier triggered, and the record names the observation that indicated the regime had changed. (BS-016-035)

(BS-016-036)
