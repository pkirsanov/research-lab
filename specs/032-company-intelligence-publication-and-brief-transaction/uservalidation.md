## Checklist

Human acceptance owns this list. Every item ships unchecked. A human checks an item only after exercising the delivered behavior.

- [ ] I can find Company Intelligence once in the catalogue and shared navigation, open its notes, and reach the same route.
- [ ] I can verify that the current company version and the Market Action brief name the same publication generation.
- [ ] I can inspect four separate horizons and see missing or stale evidence with a source, clock, reason, and affected horizon.
- [ ] I can distinguish the acknowledged pair from a refreshing, failed, dry-run, or transient browser-composition state.
- [ ] I can inspect immutable lineage, including same-day versions, predecessors, and an explicit unchanged conclusion.
- [ ] I can open the packaged route from a local file and read committed content without credentials, an account, a proxy, or a server.
- [ ] I can use the route by keyboard and at narrow width or 200 percent zoom without losing state meaning or critical provenance.
- [ ] I confirm that the public artifacts contain no holding, size, basis, profit, loss, credential, or action-authority data.

An unchecked item means human acceptance has not occurred. Automation must not alter this checklist or acceptance record.
