## Checklist

### Research coverage and preservation

- [ ] I can inspect all 23 industries and ten active company or business exposures in each, without losing historical membership or the Udemy/Skillsoft distinction.
- [ ] A selected-company refresh preserves the full universe and distinguishes refreshed, carried, failed, unreviewed and completed-event counts.
- [ ] I can open a preserved historical report or forecast with its original date and see that it was not silently rewritten.

### Evidence and causal timing

- [ ] A company dossier shows original source, publication/observation/retrieval/first-detected clocks, unit, denominator, period, entity linkage, rights and limitations.
- [ ] I can see supporting and conflicting evidence at the same time; correlated reposts do not masquerade as independent corroboration.
- [ ] An inflection window identifies dated controlling gates and does not turn water allowance, an installed asset or a job requisition into realized billable output.
- [ ] I can tell workflow efficiency, retained company economics, customer benefit, vendor capture and stock expectation apart.

### Signals, market context and events

- [ ] Next earnings appears before six-month and twelve-month milestones, with event certainty, fiscal cutoff, leading evidence, countercase, falsifier and missing monetary inputs.
- [ ] Early watch, qualified realization, surprise alert, erosion warning and abstention have visibly different labels; destination refusal leaves the owner notice visible.
- [ ] Options IV shows raw and normalized values, quote/trade clocks and quality. Invalid vendor zero is unavailable, not a claimed zero-volatility observation.
- [ ] A completed event leaves the approaching queue and retains its frozen pre-event forecast, actuals, declared return window and abstention status.

### Four-view experience

- [ ] Simple, Power, Brief and Journey identify the same current generation and research cutoff; stale or mismatched projections cannot appear current.
- [ ] Simple explains one causal chain and largest gap, while Power lets me reconstruct it from evidence, dependencies, models and assumptions.
- [ ] Every required Brief lane displays an honest empty/unavailable state when no signal qualifies.
- [ ] A local scenario marks my assumption, names affected outputs and Reset restores the evidence-derived baseline without altering Brief or history.
- [ ] Unknown, unavailable, stale, conflicted, measured zero and not-reviewed are visibly distinct in the views and exports.
- [ ] The tool remains understandable and operable on mobile, with keyboard, touch, 200% zoom and reduced motion. Units, qualifiers and source links remain accessible.
