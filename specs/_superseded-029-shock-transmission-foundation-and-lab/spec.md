| --- | --- |
| **Shock** | A sourced disruption or hypothetical disturbance with affected capacity, observed loss, uncertainty, timing, and repair path |

| **Policy action** | An authority-owned instrument with a trigger, announced amount, lifecycle, lag, reversibility, intended effects, observed effects, and evidence |
| **Actor reaction** | Observed behavior, stated intent, inferred next action, constraint, and falsifier for one modeled actor |
| **Transmission edge** | A directed relationship with sign, low/base/high magnitude, lag, persistence, evidence, causal path, refuter, and limitations |

| **Scenario curve** | A set of horizon-qualified state probabilities, calibration references, and version history |
| **Lever definition** | A definition-owned, bounded user assumption that targets one declared primitive or edge |
| **Canonical baseline** | The immutable, source-qualified topic state supplied by a validated dossier or an explicit unavailable state |
| **Model evaluation** | A deterministic evaluation over a definition, frozen evidence, declared horizons, and labeled assumptions |
| **Restoration condition** | The observation that proves a policy layer or offset restored the function it targets |
| **Finding** | A horizon-qualified claim with source, provenance, confidence basis, causal path, triggers, invalidations, refuters, and limitations |
| **Current influence** | Whether a primitive or edge may affect the current output after freshness, conflict, and refuter rules |
| **Calibration sample** | A lineage-bound set of historical outcomes that is large enough to support a realized frequency only after its declared minimum |

## Domain Capability Model

### Capability

`shock-transmission/v1` represents a source-qualified path from shocks to horizon-specific curves and findings. The path may include offsets, policy actions, actor reactions, and transmission edges. Concrete topic definitions provide domain data. The foundation provides shared semantics, lineage, validation, and honest unavailable behavior. [R1] [R3]

### Domain Primitives

| Primitive | Required business content | Lifecycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shock | Identity, source, start, affected capacity, observed loss, low/base/high uncertainty, repair path, status | proposed or observed, active, easing, ended, superseded |
| Offset | Type, target shock or edge, capacity, utilization, low/base/high effect, lag, expiry, source, limitation | available, active, exhausted, expired, refuted, superseded |
| Policy action | Owner, legal or institutional authority, trigger, instrument, amount, announcement, implementation, uptake, lag, reversibility, intended and observed effects, evidence | proposed, announced, authorized, implemented, effective, ineffective, expired, reversed, superseded |
| Actor reaction | Actor, observed behavior, stated intent, inferred next action, constraint, falsifier, evidence | observed, inferred, contradicted, stale, superseded |
| Transmission edge | From, to, sign, range, lag, persistence, evidence, refuter, limitation, overlap identity | active, constrained, stale, refuted, superseded |
| Scenario curve | Scenario states, declared horizons, probabilities, calibration, assumptions, change history | available, insufficient-sample, stale, unavailable, superseded |
| Finding | Claim, causal path, source, provenance, confidence, limitation, trigger, invalidation, horizon, lineage | current, stale, refuted, corrected, superseded |
| Lever definition | Identifier, label, target, unit, bounds, step, baseline source, provenance, topic and horizon applicability | active, unavailable, retired, superseded |
| Model evaluation | Definition identity, baseline identity, evidence cutoff, assumptions, horizons, and result digest | current, stale, unavailable, superseded |

### Relationships

- A topic definition owns zero or more shocks, offsets, policy actions, actors, edges, scenario curves, findings, and lever definitions.
- A shock may activate several offsets and actor reactions. Each relation is explicit.
- A policy action belongs to one authority. It may create an offset only through a separately declared edge and restoration condition.
- An actor reaction can create another shock, offset, or policy action. The graph preserves the originating actor and evidence.
- A transmission edge references its endpoints and any overlap identity. The same physical loss cannot be counted twice through overlapping routes.
- A scenario curve evaluates mutually exclusive states at each declared horizon. Different horizons retain distinct calibration and uncertainty.
- A finding references a path through the graph. Every projection preserves that path, its refuters, and its limitations.
- A model evaluation references canonical baselines and definition versions. A reader assumption never becomes a canonical baseline by implication.

### Business Policies

1. Reject an undeclared primitive, lever, horizon, unit, state, or edge endpoint by field path. Never infer a default. [R3] [R4]
2. Keep each topic's coefficients, levers, and horizons inside its versioned definition. [R6] [R7]
3. Apply offsets once and show their lags and expiry. Do not subtract a promised action before observed implementation or uptake. [R1]
4. Keep physical scarcity, market plumbing, liquidity, intermediation, solvency, income, and demand as distinct policy layers. [R1]
5. Keep policy institutions independent. Record coordination and legal dependencies without collapsing authority. [R1]
6. Preserve evidence roles and source classes through all computations and projections. [R1] [R3]
7. Remove a refuted or stale edge from current influence while retaining its immutable record. [R3] [R11]
8. Withhold a probability or realized rate when its declared sample or evidence coverage is insufficient. Show the sample count and reason. [R10] [R17]
9. Use owner reads for external math. A shock graph may reference an owner result but may not restate its formula. [R10] [R11] [R12] [R13]
10. Call the foundation reusable only after two unrelated topic definitions validate through it. Feature 029 authorizes no new Lab route. [R1]

## Use Cases

### UC-029-001: Admit a reusable topic definition

- **Actor:** Topic-definition steward
- **Preconditions:** A versioned topic definition declares its primitives, levers, horizons, evidence policy, and source boundaries.
- **Main Flow:** The steward validates the definition against the shared capability. Every endpoint, unit, bound, and state resolves. The accepted definition receives an immutable identity.
- **Alternative Flows:** Missing, duplicate, unknown, or incompatible members are refused by exact field path. No default is supplied.
- **Postconditions:** The topic can participate without changing the foundation or another topic's values.

### UC-029-002: Migrate the geopolitical model without rewriting history

- **Actor:** Topic-definition steward
- **Preconditions:** Feature 019 v1 records and the geopolitical v1.0.0 definition remain readable.
- **Main Flow:** The steward maps v1 shocks, flows, offsets, actors, levers, horizons, findings, and calibration into a new definition version.
- **Alternative Flows:** An unmapped v1 member blocks migration and names the missing mapping.
- **Postconditions:** Old artifacts remain byte-preserved. New outputs reference the old definition and migration identity.

### UC-029-003: Evaluate a topic through the shared foundation

- **Actor:** Recurring agenda publisher
- **Preconditions:** A topic definition and its current evidence validate.
- **Main Flow:** The publisher evaluates shocks, offsets, policies, reactions, edges, curves, and findings at the topic's declared horizons.
- **Alternative Flows:** An unavailable input withholds its dependent output. History and zero never replace it.
- **Postconditions:** The immutable review binds the exact definition, evidence cutoff, assumptions, and result identity.

### UC-029-004: Preserve independent policy actions

- **Actor:** Topic-definition steward
- **Preconditions:** Several authorities have declared policy-action records.
- **Main Flow:** The definition represents each authority's action, timing, intended effects, observed effects, and restoration condition separately.
- **Alternative Flows:** A proposed or announced action has no implemented effect unless evidence advances its lifecycle.
- **Postconditions:** Institutional ownership and cross-instrument conflicts remain visible.

### UC-029-005: Audit a causal path

- **Actor:** Evidence reviewer
- **Preconditions:** A finding references a path with evidence, refuters, and limitations.
- **Main Flow:** The reviewer opens each edge, checks source timing and provenance, and inspects active refuters and conflicts.
- **Alternative Flows:** A fired refuter removes the edge from current influence without deleting it.
- **Postconditions:** The reviewer can distinguish supported mechanism, inference, conflict, and absence.

### UC-029-006: Read a scenario curve at declared horizons

- **Actor:** Research reader
- **Preconditions:** A definition declares one or more horizon records.
- **Main Flow:** The reader compares mutually exclusive scenario states at each horizon, with calibration and uncertainty beside them.
- **Alternative Flows:** Insufficient samples withhold realized frequency. Missing inputs withhold the affected curve.
- **Postconditions:** No horizon is silently translated into `tactical`, `swing`, or `structural`.

### UC-029-007: Preserve a finding through downstream research

- **Actor:** Downstream research consumer
- **Preconditions:** A foundation finding passes a destination-free seam.
- **Main Flow:** The consumer receives identity, lineage, causal path, refuters, limitations, horizon, sources, triggers, and invalidations unchanged.
- **Alternative Flows:** Any dropped or rewritten required member refuses the projection.
- **Postconditions:** The consumer may apply its own contract without upgrading the finding's certainty.

### UC-029-008: Handle unavailable, stale, conflicting, or insufficient evidence

- **Actor:** Research reader
- **Preconditions:** At least one required input cannot support current influence.
- **Main Flow:** The reader sees the exact state, affected path, source age or conflict, and resulting withheld output.
- **Alternative Flows:** Independent unaffected paths still render.
- **Postconditions:** Absence is not converted into zero, a stale current view, or a plausible stand-in.

### UC-029-009: Inspect company relevance without inventing company facts

- **Actor:** Downstream research consumer
- **Preconditions:** A market-wide shock path names a public company or sector exposure.
- **Main Flow:** The consumer labels the path market-scoped and links to any company-qualified owner evidence.
- **Alternative Flows:** No company-qualified evidence produces an explicit unavailable company conclusion.
- **Postconditions:** The market path survives without becoming an unsupported cash-flow claim.
