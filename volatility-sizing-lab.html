What this assumes — the 1/forecastVol form holds risk steady, not growth. It coincides with growth-optimal sizing only where expected return scales with volatility, i.e. where reward-per-unit-risk is roughly stable across regimes. Under a known edge, growth-optimal sizing scales by 1/vol², not 1/vol. This tool carries no directional input and therefore no edge estimate, so it cannot test that condition and does not assert it holds.

Definitions — q is a moment order (0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0); lag is the spacing in trading days between the observations a structure function compares; ζ(q) (zeta) is the fitted scaling exponent of that structure function against lag, per order; H (the Hurst exponent) is the single common slope of ζ(q) against q·H through the origin, fit across all admitted orders. H below 0.5 indicates rougher-than-Brownian scaling in this proxy; nothing here changes the Feature 011 forecast, regime, or sizing decision above.
